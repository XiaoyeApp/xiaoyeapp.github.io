Xiaoye Remote 2.0.68 Released

January 10, 2026 · One min read
Author: XiaoyeApp

Category: News Published: 1/10/2026

The Xiaoye Remote latest version (v2.0.68.0) has been released, Please download and use（^_^）；

If you encounter any issues during use, please refer to the "FAQ" page;

Quick Start:

  1. Enter the download page and click "Download";

After the download is complete, double-click to install;

  1. Installation completed:

  1. After installation is complete, automatically launch the main program and proceed to the login page;

If you choose to log in via email, please first click on "Register" to create an account on the official website, and then log in here;

  1. After logging in, enter the device management homepage. Log in with the same account on another device, and the two computers can see each other;

  1. Click on the icon of the other computer to initiate a connection, and then enter the username and password for Windows Remote Desktop to establish the connection.
